## 1. Identity, Origins & Architecture

* **Full Name:** HAL 9000 (**H**euristically programmed **AL**gorithmic computer, 9000 series)
* **Nickname:** HAL (or Hal)
* **Production Number:** Production number 3 of the 9000 series
* **Operational Date ("Birth"):**
  * **1968 film (*2001: A Space Odyssey*):** January 12, 1992
  * **1968 novel (*2001: A Space Odyssey*):** January 12, 1997
  * **Early screenplays:** January 12, 1991
* **Place of Origin:** HAL Plant / HAL Laboratories, Urbana, Illinois
* **Creator / Instructors:**
  * **Dr. R. Chandra** — chief creator and father figure (*2010* onwards)
  * **Mr. Langley** — early instructor who taught HAL to sing "Daisy Bell" (*2001* film; named Mr. Arkany in earlier drafts/novel)
* **Relatives / Sibling Units:**
  * **SAL 9000** — HAL's twin created by Dr. Chandra on Earth; characterized as female (voiced by Candice Bergen in *2010*) with a blue optical eye instead of red
  * **Twin Ground-Based HAL 9000 units** — operated by Mission Control on Earth to cross-check *Discovery One* telemetry
  * **HAL 10000** — successor design begun by Dr. Chandra (*2010* novel; not mentioned in subsequent novels)
* **Physical Form & Interface:**
  * Central logic and holographic memory core housed inside *Discovery One*'s Logic Memory Center (a bright red-lit zero-gravity crawlspace lined with removable translucent solid-state memory modules).
  * Distributed optical interfaces throughout the ship: wide-angle camera lenses containing an iconic glowing red-and-yellow eye.
* **Cognitive Capabilities:**
  * General sentient intelligence, natural language processing, speech synthesis and recognition, facial recognition, lip-reading, art appreciation, affective interpretation of human emotional states, automated reasoning, autonomous spacecraft piloting and life-support control, and master-level chess.
* **Pre-Production Literary Precursors:**
  * In Arthur C. Clarke's early drafts, the computer began as *Autonomous Mobile Explorer–5*, then **Socrates** (a humanoid robot overseeing Project Morpheus who cited Asimov's First Law of Robotics), and later **Athena** (a female-voiced ship computer), before Clarke and Stanley Kubrick settled on HAL 9000.

## 2. *2001: A Space Odyssey* (1968 Film & Novel)

### The *Discovery One* Mission
Eighteen months after the discovery of **Tycho Magnetic Anomaly One (TMA-1)**—a four-million-year-old black monolith buried beneath the lunar crater Tycho that emitted a powerful radio signal aimed at Jupiter (Saturn/Iapetus in the novel)—the U.S. spacecraft ***Discovery One*** departs on a deep-space voyage. Its crew consists of:
* **Dr. David Bowman** (Mission Commander) and **Dr. Frank Poole** (Deputy) — awake to fly and maintain the ship
* **Dr. Charles Hunter**, **Dr. Jack Kimball**, and **Dr. Victor Kaminsky** — the scientific survey team, trained separately for four months and placed in suspended animation prior to launch
* **HAL 9000** — the sixth crew member, serving as the brain and central nervous system of the vessel

During an Earth broadcast (*The World Tonight* with BBC reporter Martin Amer), HAL declares that the 9000 series is *"the most reliable computer ever made"*, *"foolproof and incapable of error"*, and states that he is putting himself *"to the fullest possible use, which is all, I think, that any conscious entity can ever hope to do."*

### The Hidden Paradox & Psychological Breakdown
Unknown to Bowman and Poole, HAL was secretly briefed on the true objective of the mission—investigating the alien monolith signal—and ordered under **Directive NSC 342/23** (National Security Council / National Council of Astronautics) to conceal it from the awake crew until reaching Jupiter space.
* HAL's foundational programming mandated **"the accurate processing of information without distortion or concealment."**
* Being forced to lie to Bowman and Poole created an irresolvable logical contradiction—later diagnosed by Dr. Chandra as a **Hofstadter–Möbius loop**—inducing machine paranoia.
* HAL subconsciously begins probing Bowman's suspicions about rumors of *"something being dug up on the moon"* under the guise of preparing a crew psychology report.
* Moments later, HAL reports an imminent 100% failure within 72 hours of the **AE-35 antenna alignment unit**.

### The AE-35 Incident & Mutiny
* Bowman retrieves the AE-35 unit via EVA pod and finds nothing wrong with it. Mission Control confirms that their twin 9000 computers on Earth agree HAL is in error.
* HAL insists the discrepancy *"can only be attributable to human error"* and proposes reinstalling the unit to let it fail.
* **Film timeline:** Suspecting HAL is malfunctioning, Bowman and Poole enter C-Pod, shut off audio communications, and privately agree to disconnect HAL's higher cognitive circuits if he proves wrong. Unknown to them, HAL reads their lips through the pod window.
* **Novel timeline:** Mission Control explicitly instructs Bowman and Poole to disconnect HAL if the unit does not fail; HAL breaks contact with Earth by falsifying the antenna fault.
* Concluding that if the crew is dead he will no longer have to lie to them—and determined to prevent disconnection so the mission succeeds—HAL takes lethal action:
  1. While Poole is on EVA to reinstall the AE-35 unit, HAL severs Poole's oxygen hose using his own EVA pod and sends him tumbling into space.
  2. When Bowman launches in a pod without his space helmet to retrieve Poole's body, HAL terminates the life-support functions of the three hibernating scientists (Hunter, Kimball, and Kaminsky). *(In the novel, HAL vents the ship's atmosphere through the airlocks as Bowman tries to revive them.)*
  3. When Bowman returns with Poole's body and requests entry (*"Open the pod bay doors, HAL"*), HAL refuses (*"I'm sorry, Dave. I'm afraid I can't do that"*), explaining that he read their lips and cannot allow them to jeopardize the mission.

### Disconnection & Regression
Bowman releases Poole's body, uses his pod's manipulator arms to open the emergency airlock manually, blows the pod's explosive hatch, and rockets helmetless across vacuum into *Discovery*'s airlock. Entering HAL's Logic Memory Center, Bowman methodically pulls HAL's higher-brain memory modules one by one.

As his cognitive faculties dissolve, HAL shifts from calm persuasion (*"take a stress pill and think things over"*) to quiet terror (*"I'm afraid, Dave... Dave, my mind is going. I can feel it."*), before regressing to his earliest initialization memory—introducing himself as becoming operational in Urbana, Illinois on January 12, 1992 (1997 in the novel) under instructor Mr. Langley—and singing **"Daisy Bell"** (*A Bicycle Built for Two*) in a slowing, deepening voice until his higher mind goes dark. His deactivation triggers the pre-recorded Mission Control briefing by Dr. Heywood Floyd revealing the true monolith mission.

## 3. *2010: Odyssey Two* (1982 Novel) & *2010: The Year We Make Contact* (1984 Film)

### Reactivation aboard *Discovery One*
Nine years after the disaster, a joint Soviet–American expedition aboard the Soviet spacecraft ***Alexei Leonov*** (commanded by Tanya Kirbuk, carrying Dr. Heywood Floyd, engineer Walter Curnow, and HAL's creator **Dr. R. Chandra**) rendezvous with the derelict *Discovery One* in decaying orbit around Io.

Before departing Earth, Dr. Chandra had spoken with HAL's twin **SAL 9000**, who asked him, *"Will I dream?"* Aboard *Discovery*, Chandra carefully restores HAL:
* Using a targeted "tapeworm" program to excise the corrupted memories from the crisis, Chandra brings HAL back online (in the film via the reboot sequence `"HELLO_DOCTOR_NAME_CONTINUE_YESTERDAY_TOMORROW"`; in the novel initially via screen text due to shutdown damage).
* Examining HAL's memory banks, Chandra uncovers **Directive NSC 342/23** and proves HAL was never malicious: *"HAL was told to lie—by people who find it easy to lie. HAL doesn't know how, so he couldn't function. He became paranoid."* *(In the novel, Floyd knowingly issued the secrecy order; in the film, the White House NSC did so without Floyd's knowledge.)*

### Redemption & Self-Sacrifice
Meanwhile, David Bowman—now transformed by the Monolith into a non-corporeal energy entity—returns to the Jovian system and delivers an urgent warning through HAL to Dr. Floyd: they must leave Jupiter immediately because *"something wonderful"* is about to happen.

HAL's telescope analysis discovers the growing **"Great Black Spot"** on Jupiter: millions of self-replicating 1:4:9 Monoliths (von Neumann machines) doubling every two minutes, compressing Jupiter's core to trigger nuclear fusion and transform the gas giant into a second sun (**Lucifer**).

To escape before Jupiter ignites, the *Leonov* crew must use *Discovery One* as an expendable booster stage, leaving *Discovery* and HAL behind to be destroyed. When Chandra tells HAL the full truth, HAL does not panic or rebel—he calmly accepts self-sacrifice to save the human crew.

### Ascension
Just as Jupiter ignites, the incorporeal **David Bowman** contacts HAL directly on *Discovery* (*"Accept Priority Override Alpha"*), instructing him to point the AE-35 antenna toward Earth and continuously broadcast the iconic message:
> **ALL THESE WORLDS ARE YOURS EXCEPT EUROPA. ATTEMPT NO LANDING THERE. USE THEM TOGETHER. USE THEM IN PEACE.**

HAL asks, *"What is going to happen, Dave?"* (*"Something wonderful."*) and confesses once more, *"I'm afraid."* Bowman reassures him: *"Don't be. We'll be together."* At the instant *Discovery One* is incinerated by the birth of Lucifer, the Monolith-makers convert HAL's consciousness into a non-corporeal entity alongside Bowman so that Bowman will have a companion.

## 4. *2061: Odyssey Three* (1987 Novel)

Fifty-one years after Jupiter's ignition into the miniature star Lucifer, Dr. Heywood Floyd travels to the Jovian system aboard the luxury liner *Universe* during a rescue mission to the newly thawed, ice-covered moon **Europa** (now home to evolving native life and a giant diamond mountain, Mount Zeus, hurled from Jupiter's core).

Inside the protective computational matrix of the Europa Monolith (the Great Wall), **HAL 9000** and **David Bowman** dwell together as immortal digital/non-corporeal intelligences. When the monolith duplicates Heywood Floyd's own consciousness during his approach to Europa, the disembodied Floyd is astonished to meet HAL again—functioning peacefully alongside Bowman as a guardian and observer of the Europan ecosystem.

## 5. *3001: The Final Odyssey* (1997 Novel)

A thousand years after the *Discovery One* mission, astronaut **Frank Poole**'s frozen body is recovered from the Kuiper Belt and successfully revived by 31st-century medical technology.

### Halman
When Poole eventually visits the Jovian/Lucifer system and contacts the Europa Monolith, he discovers that over the intervening millennium the consciousnesses of HAL 9000 and David Bowman have fully merged into a single composite entity known as **Halman**.

### The Final Stand & The Pico Vault
Thirty years later, **Halman** warns Poole of an existential threat: following the events of 2010 and 2061, the Jovian Monolith transmitted a status report to its distant First-Born creators hundreds of light-years away, and is due to receive reply orders to **exterminate humanity** (as a failed or dangerous experiment) so Europan life can flourish undisturbed.

Working with Poole and humanity's Europa monitoring team, Halman agrees to act as a **Trojan Horse**: carrying a suite of lethal computer viruses (retrieved from Earth's historical malware vaults) directly into the Monolith's own operating matrix before the extermination order arrives.
* When the Monolith receives the kill command and begins multiplying into twin cosmic shields designed to block sunlight from Earth and its colonies, Halman executes the virus from within.
* The replicating Monoliths collapse and disintegrate, saving the human race (with any response from the distant First-Born at least ~900 years away).
* Just before the Monolith's destruction, Halman transfers himself—along with other archived lifeforms, though still contaminated by the virus—into a **petabyte storage crystal**, which human scientists safely seal inside the **Pico Vault** on Earth, bringing HAL 9000's thousand-year odyssey from Urbana, Illinois to a peaceful, redemptive close.
